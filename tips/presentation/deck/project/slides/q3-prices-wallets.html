Source: paper Section 5.3, Appendix E
Speaker: Q3 speaker. About 1 minute. Why: Q1 uses the price and Q2 uses wallet volume, so we check their shapes too. How: a histogram of the price one day before close, and a QQ plot of wallet volume, raw and log. Result: prices pile up near both ends of the scale, with a mean of 0.376 and a median of 0.320. Wallet volume is strongly skewed. Makers have a mean of 14,103 USDC against a median of 101.2, and takers have 5,339 against 86.6. Meaning: the same long-tail shape appears in the prices and in the wallets. We report it without a verdict.
