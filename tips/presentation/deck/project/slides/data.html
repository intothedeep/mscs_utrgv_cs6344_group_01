Speaker 1 (Taek Lim). About 1 minute. Why: a price is only useful if it exists before the market closes and the outcome is clear, so we filter. How: we use Polymarket-v1, a public database of 1.2 billion trades and 1.3 million markets from November 2022 to April 2026. In scope are 688,131 standard yes/no markets. Each bar shows what is left after one rule. We remove 255,420 Up/Down markets, which settle in a different way. We remove 62,718 with no clear winner. We remove 215,289 with no price one day before the close, 80,230 whose price is older than 24 hours, and 26,526 with fewer than 10 trades. We fixed these rules before we saw any Q1 result. Result: 47,948 markets are left, 7.0% of the scope. Meaning: all three questions use the same 47,948 markets, so the three answers fit together. The results hold for markets that pass these filters.
