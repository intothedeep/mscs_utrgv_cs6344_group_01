Why markets drop out of the 688,131 in scope
Source: paper, Data section
Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 1 minute. The database holds 688,131 markets in scope. We keep only 47,948 of them, which is 7.0%. A market must have a clear winner, a price one day before it closes, and at least 10 trades. The three biggest reasons for dropping are Up/Down markets, no price one day before the close, and a stale price. All three questions use the same 47,948 kept markets.
