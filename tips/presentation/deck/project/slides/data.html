Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 1 minute. Why: a price is only useful if it exists before the market closes and the outcome is clear, so we filter. How: a market needs a clear winner, a price one day before it closes, and at least 10 trades. We fixed these rules before we saw any Q1 result. Result: the database holds 688,131 markets in scope and we keep 47,948, which is 7.0%. The biggest drops are Up/Down markets at 37.1%, no price one day before the close at 31.3%, and a stale price at 11.7%. Meaning: all three questions use the same 47,948 markets, so the three answers fit together. The result holds for markets that pass the filters.
