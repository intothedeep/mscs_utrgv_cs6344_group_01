Source: README section 1
Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 30 seconds. Why: before we test a claim, we must say what the claim is. How: a YES share on Polymarket pays one dollar if the event happens and nothing if it does not. If the share costs 70 cents, readers take that as a 70% chance. Result: this is a reading, not a measured fact. Meaning: if the reading is wrong, everyone who uses the price as a probability is misled. Reading a price as a probability is exactly the claim we test.
