When Polymarket Says 70%,
Does It Happen 70% of the Time?

Group 1 · Team Brier

Taek Lim · Zhugang Liu (Tony) · Francisco Cortez

CS 6344 Introduction to Data Science
The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley (UTRGV)

Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 30 seconds. Good morning. We are Group 1, Team Brier. Our question is simple: when Polymarket shows a price of 70%, does that event happen about 70% of the time? We answer it on real trade data, and each of us will present one of our three research questions.
