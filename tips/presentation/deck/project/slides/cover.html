Speaker 1 (Taek Lim). About 20 seconds. Good morning. We are Group 1, Team PolyHindSight. Our topic is prediction markets. Our question is simple: when a market shows a price of 70%, does that event happen about 70% of the time? We answer it on real trade data, and each of us will present one of our three research questions.
