Speaker: Q2 speaker. About 1 minute. We ranked wallets by their volume in USDC, a dollar-pegged token, separately for makers and takers. The top 20% of wallets hold 98.9% of maker volume and 98.0% of taker volume. The 80/20 rule would predict 80%, so the concentration is far beyond it. The top 1% alone hold 86.1% of maker volume and 75.9% of taker volume. A wallet is one address, not one person, and we describe this without a verdict.
