Source: paper Section 5.2, Appendix D.3
Speaker: Q2 speaker. About 1 minute. Why: volume tells us who trades, but not who gains. So we ask how much each wallet won or lost. How: for each wallet we add its cash flow and its payout at resolution over the kept markets, then subtract the recorded taker fee. Result: before fees, wallets won 389.8 million USDC and lost the same amount, so the markets are zero-sum. The median wallet loses about 20 cents. 871,667 wallets lose and 663,072 win, out of 1,569,986. The top 1% of wallets take an estimated 88.0% of all winnings. For comparison, the top 1% of makers hold 86.1% of maker volume. Meaning: winnings are concentrated like volume. These numbers are estimates. Some trades are matched by a mint or a merge, with no wallet on the other side, and we do not model that. We also do not report fee totals, because the meaning of the fee column is not verified. This result is descriptive and outside our pre-registered Q2 criteria.
