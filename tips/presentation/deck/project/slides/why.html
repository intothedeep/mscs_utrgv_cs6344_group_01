A price is useful only if it can be read as a probability

A YES share pays $1 if the event happens. At a price of $0.70, readers see a 70% chance. Is that chance right?

Q1
Is the crowd right?

Q3

Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 1 minute. A YES share on Polymarket pays one dollar if the event happens. So a price of 70 cents is read as a 70% chance. We ask whether that reading is right. Q1 tests it directly, price range by price range. Q2 asks who the crowd is, and Q3 describes what the betting itself looks like.
