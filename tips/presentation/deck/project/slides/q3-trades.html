Trade sizes: a few huge trades pull the mean far up

What: we pool all 47,948 kept markets and look at the shape of price, trade size and wallet volume, not each market alone.

Why: with a long tail, the mean misleads. The shape tells us which summary to trust.

Raw points bend away, log points follow the line

Source: paper Section 5.3
Speaker: Q3 speaker. About 1 minute. Why: trade size is the basic unit of betting, so its shape tells us what a typical trade is. How: a histogram, and a QQ plot against the normal, raw and after a log transform. Result: the mean trade size is 129.8 USDC but the median is only 6.9. In the QQ plot the raw points bend away from the straight line and the log points follow it much better. The normal fit has a KS statistic of 0.469, which is a poor fit. Meaning: a few very large trades pull the mean far above the typical trade, so the median and the log scale describe the data better. We report this without a verdict.
