Speaker 1 (Taek Lim). About 45 seconds. Why: the hook gave us one question. If a share costs 70 cents, is it really a 70% chance? We look at it in three ways. Click. Q1 asks whether the crowd is right. In this sketch the dots sit just under the line where price equals how often things happen. That is the test: do events priced at 70% happen about 70% of the time? Click. Q2 asks who the crowd is. Does a small group of wallets hold most of the money? If so, the price may reflect a few traders, but volume is not price-setting power. Click. Q3 asks what betting looks like. When sizes have a long right tail, the mean moves far from the median, so we must pick the honest summary number. These sketches show shapes only.
