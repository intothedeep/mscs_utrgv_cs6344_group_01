Backup. Speaker: Q1 speaker. Outcome 1 is the first-listed outcome of a market. In 63.9% of the kept markets, or 30,630, it is labeled Yes. A gate review found that saying Yes is priced too high named a label that no saved file showed. So the paper says outcome 1 instead. Among the Yes-labeled markets alone, nine of nine bins from 0 to 8 are below the price, with gaps of 0.4 to 5.6 percentage points. This is descriptive and adds no new test.
