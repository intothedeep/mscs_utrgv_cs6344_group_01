Source: paper Section 5.1
Speaker: Q1 speaker. About 1 minute 15 seconds. Why: a verdict of not calibrated does not say which way the prices miss, so we read the bins. How: the chart plots the observed rate against the mean price in each bin. A point on the diagonal would be perfect. Result: in nine of the ten bins the observed rate is below the mean price. Only the top bin, from 90 to 100%, is not. Over all 47,948 markets the net gap is minus 0.99%. The biggest gap is in the 70 to 80% bin, where the price is 74.6% and the observed rate is 71.3%. Meaning: the effect is small, but it runs in one direction. Outcome 1 is priced a little too high.
