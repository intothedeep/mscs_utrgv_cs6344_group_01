Speaker: Q2 speaker. About 1 minute 30 seconds. Why: the crowd has two roles, makers and takers, so we check whether they differ. How: we compare log volume with a Welch t-test and a KS test, and we correlate trade count with volume using Spearman. The figure shows, for each role, the wallet distribution, the volume distribution and the Lorenz curve, with the top 20% in color. Result: the Welch t statistic is 117.1. The difference in mean log10 volume is 0.219, makers 2.104 and takers 1.885. The KS statistic is 0.057, and the Spearman correlation between trade count and volume is 0.655. Mean volume sits far above median volume in both roles. Makers have 14,103 against a median of 101.2 USDC, and takers have 5,339 against 86.6. Meaning: both roles are strongly skewed, and the volume sits with a few wallets in each. The Welch p-value is a float-underflow bound, not an exact value.
