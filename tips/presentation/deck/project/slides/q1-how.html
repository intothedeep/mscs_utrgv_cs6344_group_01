Panels 1 and 2: dots scaled. Source: paper Section 4.2, Table 1
Speaker 2 (Q1). About 1 minute 15 seconds. Four steps, one per click. Step 1: we drop all 47,948 markets into 10 bins by price, from 0 to 1. Each dot is about a thousand markets. Most markets sit in the cheapest bin. Click. Step 2: we compare the price with what happened. Take the 70 to 80% bin and shrink it to 100 markets. The price says about 75 should happen. Only 71 did. The four orange circles are the missing ones. In real numbers, we expected 1,716 and saw 1,640. Click. Step 3: one chi-square test adds up the misses of all 10 bins. If prices were right, the value would sit near 10, and 18.3 is the 5% line. Ours is 68.0, far off the chart. So prices are not calibrated as a whole. Click. Step 4: a binomial test in each bin says where. For bin 7, seeing 1,640 when we expect 1,716 has a chance of only 0.00036. Ten tests need a correction, so we use Benjamini-Hochberg, and 5 of 10 bins are off. We fixed these rules before we saw any result.
