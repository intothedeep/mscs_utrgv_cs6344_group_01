Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 45 seconds. We asked three questions, so here are three answers. Q1: is the crowd right? Almost. On 47,948 markets the net gap is minus 0.99%. Events happen slightly less often than priced. Q2: who is the crowd? A few wallets. The top 20% of wallets hold 98.9% of maker volume, and the top 1% take an estimated 88.0% of all winnings. Q3: what does a trade look like? Small. The median trade is 6.9 USDC, while the mean is 129.8, so the median describes a typical trade. So the takeaway is simple. You can read a Polymarket price as a probability, if you read it a little lower. Next, what this study cannot say, and what we would do next.
