Back to the hook: being right often is not the same as being calibrated.

Speaker: Intro (Taek Lim). About 1 minute. Why: we started with one question, so we end with its answer. How: we read Q1, Q2 and Q3 together. Result: can a Polymarket price be read as a probability? Not exactly. On 47,948 markets the prices are not calibrated as a whole. The net gap is minus 0.99%, which is small but runs in one direction. By volume, the top 20% of wallets hold 98.9% of maker volume. Trade sizes are strongly right skewed, with a mean of 129.8 USDC and a median of 6.9. Meaning: back to the news story. A market can be right most of the time and still have prices that are not calibrated. In our data it is right 83.4% of the time at the 0.5 line, and the prices still miss. These are measured patterns, not proof of cause.
