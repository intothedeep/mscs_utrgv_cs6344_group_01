Backup. Speaker: Q1 speaker. With almost 48,000 markets, even a tiny gap gives a tiny p-value, so we read the effect size first. The net gap is minus 0.99%, and the largest per-bin gap is 3.29 points. We set no size line before seeing results, so calling it small is our reading. Check M5 adds a practical line, the mean half spread of 1.57%. The largest implied gap is 2.22%, above the line as a point estimate. But a price passes only when its whole 95% bootstrap band lies beyond the line, and 0 of 7 grid prices do. So we cannot say the gap is surely larger than the cost of trading.
