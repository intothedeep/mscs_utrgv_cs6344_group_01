Speaker: Q3 speaker. About 1 minute. Q3 only describes the data. The mean trade size is 129.8 USDC, but the median is only 6.9. So a few very large trades pull the mean far above the typical trade. In the QQ plot, the raw points bend away from the straight line, and after a log transform they follow it much better. Prices pile up near both ends of the scale, with a mean of 0.376 and a median of 0.320. We report this without a verdict.
