Speaker 1 (Taek Lim). About 45 seconds. Why: some of you may not know what a prediction market is, so we start there. How: a prediction market asks a yes-or-no question, for example, will it rain on Sunday. You can buy a YES share. It pays one dollar if the answer is yes and nothing if not. People trade these shares, and the price moves. If a YES share costs 70 cents, people read that as a 70% chance. The values on this slide are only an example. Result: the two biggest markets in the US are Kalshi, a US exchange regulated by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, and Polymarket, a crypto-based exchange that trades in USDC, a dollar-pegged token. To give a sense of size: Kalshi traded 23.8 billion dollars in 2025. That is about three times the ticket sales of Powerball and Mega Millions together, about 8 billion dollars in the last fiscal year. One caution: trading volume counts each contract at one dollar, and the same money can trade many times, so this is a rough sense of size, not an exact comparison. Meaning: our data come from Polymarket.
