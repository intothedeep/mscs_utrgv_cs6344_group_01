Q1 result: prices are not calibrated as a whole

Chi-square goodness of fit on 10 price bins: the prices do not match what happened.
χ² = 68.0
10 degrees of freedom, 47,948 markets
p = 1.1×10⁻¹⁰
assumes independent markets, so it may be too small
9 of 10 bins below 0
Source: paper Section 5.1
Speaker: Q1 speaker. About 1 minute 15 seconds. Why: this is the test we fixed before the results, so its answer is the Q1 verdict. How: it compares the observed counts in the 10 bins with the counts the prices predict. Result: the chi-square statistic is 68.0 with 10 degrees of freedom, and p is about 1.1 times ten to the minus ten. The verdict is that prices are not calibrated as a whole. The chart shows the gap in each bin, the observed rate minus the mean price. Nine of the ten bars are below zero. The p-value assumes independent markets, so it may be too small. Meaning: the prices do not match what happened, and the next slide shows how and by how much.
