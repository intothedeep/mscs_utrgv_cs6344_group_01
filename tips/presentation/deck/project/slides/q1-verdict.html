Q1: prices are not calibrated as a whole

Chi-square goodness of fit on 10 price bins: the prices do not match what happened.
χ² = 68.0
10 degrees of freedom, 47,948 markets
p = 1.1×10⁻¹⁰
assumes independent markets, so it may be too small
3.29 points
largest per-bin gap (70 to 80% bin): small
Gap by price bin: observed rate minus mean price
Source: paper Section 5.1
Speaker: Q1 speaker. About 1 minute. We split the prices into ten bins and compare the observed rate of outcome 1 with the mean price in each bin. A chi-square goodness of fit test compares observed counts with the counts the prices predict. We fixed this rule before seeing any result. The statistic is 68.0 with p about ten to the minus ten, so the verdict is that prices are not calibrated as a whole. The gap is small: the largest per-bin gap is 3.29 points, in the 70 to 80% bin. The the p-value assumes independent markets, so it may be too small.
