Speaker 1 (Taek Lim). About 45 seconds. Why: we want to show how people use these markets. How: this is a real Kalshi market from November 2026. The question is: will the U.S. confirm that aliens exist? Result: more than 44 million dollars have been traded on this one question. The price for "before January 20, 2029" is 15%. That means the crowd thinks it happens about one time in seven. Meaning: a price shows us what other people believe, and they back it with money. So the next question is: can we trust a price like this as a real chance? This market is not in our data. We use it only as an example.
