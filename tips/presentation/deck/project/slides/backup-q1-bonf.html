Backup, Q1 speaker. About 30 seconds. Bonferroni guards the chance of even one false finding across our ten bin tests. It splits the 5% level over ten tests, so every bin uses the same cut of 0.005. A bin is rejected when its p-value is under that cut. Four bins pass: 0, 7, 1 and 6. All four are priced above what happened. It is strict: bin 8, with p equal to 0.018, does not pass.
