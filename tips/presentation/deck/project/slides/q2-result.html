Source: paper Section 5.2
Speaker: Q2 speaker. About 1 minute 15 seconds. Why: we want to see how far volume is concentrated compared with a familiar reference. How: we rank wallets by USDC volume and read the shares. The left chart is the CCDF and the middle chart is the Lorenz curve. Result: the top 20% of wallets hold 98.9% of maker volume and 98.0% of taker volume. The 80/20 rule would predict 80.0%. The top 1% alone hold 86.1% of maker volume and 75.9% of taker volume. In the CCDF the tail bends downward, so it is not a straight line. The Lorenz curve sits far below the equality line. Meaning: this far exceeds the 80/20 rule. We describe it without a pre-registered verdict.
