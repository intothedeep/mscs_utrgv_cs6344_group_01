- Supported answer (descriptive): the price one day before the close is U-shaped (median 0.32, q25 0.015, q75 0.66, mean 0.376). Trade size is strongly right skewed (median 6.93 USDC, mean 129.8 USDC, q99 1,955, max 798,400 on a deterministic sample of 1,000,000 trades). Wallet volume is also right skewed (median 91.1 USDC, mean 7,745 USDC). Of the three fitted models, the power law on the top 10% is the closest for both trade size (alpha 1.875, KS D 0.0388) and wallet volume (alpha 1.788, KS D 0.0239). Normal and exponential fits have KS D above 0.46.
- Files and keys: `runs/142082_mvp1_full_v1r3/q3_results.json` `summary.p_1d`, `summary.trade_size_usdc`, `summary.wallet_volume_usdc`, `fits.*`, `violin_p1d_by_category`. Figures: `fig1_fig2_hist.png`, `ecdf.png`, `fig3_qq.png`, `fig_violin_p1d_by_category.png`.
