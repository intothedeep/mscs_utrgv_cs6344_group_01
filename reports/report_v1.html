How to read

  • Pre-registered line: chi-square p < 0.05 means prices are not calibrated as a whole.
  • A bin is off when its BH-corrected p < 0.05. A negative difference means the event happened less often than priced.
  • Effect w is Cohen's w. About 0.1 is small. A tiny p with a small w is a real but small gap.
  • Brier is the mean squared error of the price. 0.25 is a coin flip at 0.5, 0 is perfect.

Method notes and reproduce

Method notes and limits

  • KS p-values are optimistic when parameters are fitted to the same data.
  • K-means is a side analysis. The clusters describe the sample.
  • A wallet is an address, not a person.
  • Tests assume independent markets. Markets on one event are not independent.
  • Duplicate rows are kept. Volume includes trades after T - 1 day.
  • Q1 covers only markets with a trade at least 1 day before T. 215,289 markets (31.3% of scope) have none and are dropped (no_price).
  • no_outcome (62,718 markets) includes markets whose outcome_seq=1 label never traded.
  • Pre-registered verdicts exist only for scope = full runs.
